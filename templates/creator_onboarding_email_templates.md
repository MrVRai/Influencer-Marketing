## 📋 Recommended Google Form Fields for Creator Onboarding:

1. **Full Name**
2. **Instagram Profile URL / Handle** (e.g. `@username`)
3. **Follower Count** (Dropdown: `<10K`, `10K–50K`, `50K–100K`, `100K+`)
4. **Primary Content Niche** (Checkboxes: *Skincare, Makeup/Beauty, Haircare, Fashion/Lifestyle, Wellness*)
5. **WhatsApp Number** (Crucial for dispatch verification)
6. **Email Address**
7. **Complete Courier Shipping Address with Pincode** (For instant product dispatch)
8. **Commercial Rate Card** (Optional: *Only for paid campaigns — Reel Rate / Story Rate*)
