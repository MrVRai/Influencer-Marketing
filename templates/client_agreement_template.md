### 3. CONTENT APPROVAL & REVISIONS WORKFLOW

1. **Draft Review:** The Agency will share raw drafts/scripts of creator content with the Client for review prior to publishing.
2. **Approval Window:** The Client must provide written approval or feedback within **48 hours** of draft receipt. If no feedback is received within 48 hours, the draft will be deemed approved.
3. **Revisions Limit:** Includes **one (1) round of minor edits** (e.g., text overlay, caption tweak, logo size). Major reshoots due to changes not specified in the original brief will attract additional production charges.

---

### 4. USAGE RIGHTS & WHITELISTING

1. **Organic Posting:** Deliverables will remain published on the creators' organic profile grids indefinitely (or minimum 90 days for feed posts, 24 hours for Stories).
2. **Paid Ads & Whitelisting:** Paid digital advertising (running Meta Ads through creator handles) or commercial TV/OOH usage is **not included** unless explicitly stated and budgeted as a separate usage license.

---

### 5. CANCELLATION & TERMINATION

1. If the Client terminates the campaign after signing and before creator production begins, a **15% cancellation fee** will apply.
2. If the Client terminates the campaign after creators have started production/filming, the **50% advance payment will be forfeited** to cover creator reservation and production expenses.
